# Project Discussion

## Decisions

- Project details and their discussions are publicly readable; posting, editing, deleting, replying, and reacting require authentication.
- Comments are plain text up to 1,000 characters. Replies stop at one level; a reply cannot receive another reply.
- Comment authors alone may edit or delete their comments. Deleting a parent comment cascades to its replies and reactions.
- Reactions use the fixed set `👍`, `💡`, `🎉`, and `❤️`. A user may toggle each emoji once per comment, including on their own comment.
- There are no author notifications, mentions, rich text, reporting queue, or project-owner moderation controls.

## Why

Comments add durable feedback beside the project without turning the board into a general social network. Plain text, one reply level, fixed reactions, and author ownership keep the reading and moderation surface bounded on mobile and in a small trusted course community.

## Reconsider when

- Abuse appears or the audience grows beyond the trusted course community.
- Authors repeatedly miss useful feedback and passive revisit behavior is insufficient.
- Longer discussions require deeper threading, mentions, or richer formatting.
